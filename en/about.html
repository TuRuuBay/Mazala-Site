Mazala Outdoors is a premium outdoor equipment distributor based in Ulaanbaatar. We hold exclusive Mongolian distribution rights for Greenstove (pellet and wood stoves), Coody (inflatable tents) and Sofirn (lights), and official distribution for Bereg, OneTigris, Acebeam and Civivi.

The Mongol Shalguur standard

Every product is field-tested in real Mongolian conditions — winter temperatures below −30°C, steppe wind, multi-week expeditions. Each published test report documents at least one flaw, because a report without a flaw is a report you can't trust. Tests are filmed in full on the TuRuu Bay YouTube channel.
